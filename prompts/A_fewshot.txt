EXAMPLE 1:

ACQUIRER PROFILE:
Company: [Redacted], FY2004
Core Business: Enterprise database software and middleware. Market leader in relational databases for large corporations.
Stated Strategy: Expand into enterprise applications to compete with SAP end-to-end. 
Key Financials: Revenue $10.2B, EBITDA margin 38%, Market cap $58B

CORRECT TARGETS:
1. PeopleSoft — HR and ERP software, fills the application gap the acquirer lacks
2. Siebel Systems — CRM leader, complements database with front-office applications
3. BEA Systems — Middleware and application servers, strengthens platform integration
4. Hyperion Solutions — Financial planning software, expands into the CFO suite
5. Retek — Retail ERP, adds vertical specialization
